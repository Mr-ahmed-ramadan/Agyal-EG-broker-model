The Agyal logo and how to use it

شعار أجيال وطريقة استخدامه

أجيال means generations. The mark is three arches standing one inside the next, the innermost lit in bronze: something built by one generation and walked through by the ones after it. Everything here is free to download for press, partners and anyone writing about Agyal.

Logo with name

The name sits on the arch's floor. Cormorant Garamond for Agyal, Amiri for أجيال — a revival of the Bulaq Press type cut in Cairo. The letters are outlined, so the files look right without either font installed.

Please don't

  • Recolour the mark, or swap the bronze doorway for any other colour.
  • Stretch, tilt, outline, or add shadows, gradients or effects.
  • Put the colour version on a dark ground — use ivory instead.
  • Crowd it: keep clear space on every side at least the height of the bronze doorway.
  • Retype the name in another font. Use the files as they are.

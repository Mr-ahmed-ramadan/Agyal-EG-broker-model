The Agyal universe

Where Agyal sits, and how everything connects. Agyal is the platform behind a licensed broker: it powers the client app and the back office, and links the broker to banks, market venues, custody and the service providers around a trade. Money and securities never sit with Agyal.

How to read it

  • Agyal is the middle layer, not the front. The investor sees the broker's brand; the broker's team works in the console. Both run on Agyal.
  • The broker stays the licensed party under the FRA. Agyal is the technology and operations behind it, and never contracts with clients.
  • Paper is sourced from the banks (primary dealers) over FIX, with the best price shown to the client. G-FIT · EGX is the exchange's secondary venue and a future execution route the same engine can plug into.
  • Money and securities stay outside Agyal. Client cash sits only in the broker's segregated account; every paper is registered to the client's own unified code at MCDR or the CBE.
  • The service providers around a trade — eKYC/AML, SMS and email — connect through Agyal but are the broker's chosen, licensed providers.

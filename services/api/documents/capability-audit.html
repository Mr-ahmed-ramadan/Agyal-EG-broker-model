Capability audit & gap to production

An honest view of what the platform does today, what is simulated in the demo, and what must be done before a broker handles real client money. Written against the code rather than from memory: every claim here was checked in the repository on the date above. Where the demo and a document disagree, the code wins and the document is wrong.

3. Hardening before real money

ItemStateWhy it matters
KYC evidence capture and retentionGapThe largest build item. No upload, no storage, no retention, no schema fields for an ID image, a selfie or a liveness recording. Which evidence must be kept is one of the questions put to the Authority.
Egyptian public-holiday calendarGapSettlement and maturity dates skip weekends only, so every date around a public holiday is wrong.
Tax treatment per instrumentGapA flat 20% on yield is applied; needs tax adviser sign-off (T-bills vs bonds, corporate vs sovereign, who withholds).
Monitoring and alertingGapThere is no observability. The health endpoint returns a fixed "ok" without checking the database, the FIX session or the outbox, and a stuck outbox is noticed by a person looking at a screen. Needs real health checks, alerting on FIX session loss, stuck outbox and reconciliation breaks, plus metrics and log aggregation.
Backups, restore drills, disaster recoveryGapNothing is in place. Single region; no backup policy and no restore has been rehearsed.
Secrets and key managementGapField-encryption keys are an environment variable, not a managed key. No rotation path.
Rate limiting beyond the public pagesGapIn memory and per instance, applied only to public endpoints. It does not survive horizontal scaling, and authenticated endpoints have none. One-time codes are the exception: those limits are in the database and are real.
Demo switches offGapThe hosted environment runs with demo mode on (coupons can be confirmed before their date) and demo logins on (codes shown on screen for the published demo accounts). Both are deliberate and scoped, and both must be off before any real money.
Staff authenticationGapEmail codes today. Rotating refresh tokens, device binding and an authenticator app or hardware key for staff are outstanding.
Independent security review and penetration testGapRequired by brokers and good practice before go-live. No dependency, secret or static analysis scanning in CI either.
Data residency and hostingGapHosted in Europe today. Whether Egyptian residency is mandatory is an open question with the Authority, and the answer decides the production region. Infrastructure as code once the provider is chosen.
Regulatory reporting packsGapFRA and AML reporting formats agreed with the pilot broker.
Client-facing legal documentsGapTerms, risk disclosure and privacy notice are configured as links to documents that do not exist yet. The broker's, reviewed by counsel.
Data protection pack (Law 151/2020)GapProcessor terms, retention schedule, residency position and breach procedure. Drafted, not settled.
Row-level security, audit trail, maker-checker, step-up codesRealIn place and covered by tests. Tenant isolation is enforced by the database itself, not only by the API.

4. The gap to a production version

Three different kinds of work, and they are worth keeping apart: one is a contract, one is engineering, and one is someone else's answer.

Swap a stand-in for a counterparty

Each of these already sits behind a named interface with a working implementation on the other side, exercised end to end on every build. Connecting the real one is an integration and a contract, not a redesign: partner banks over FIX, eKYC, AML screening, MCDR and custody, SMS, deposit confirmation, indicative rates. This is the honest strength of the build and the thing worth saying first.

Build, because nothing stands in for it

  1. KYC evidence capture and storage — upload, encryption, retention, and the audit of who looked at it. Nothing exists today.
  2. Egyptian public-holiday calendar — settlement and maturity arithmetic.
  3. Bank-statement import with auto-matching — the designed deposit path, not yet written.
  4. Observability — real health checks, alerting, metrics, log aggregation.
  5. Backups and a rehearsed restore, then a disaster-recovery position.
  6. Managed key store with rotation, and rate limiting that survives more than one instance.
  7. Non-FIX bank adapters, so no connection mode can silently drop an order.
  8. Regulatory reporting and the client-facing legal pack.

Blocked on an answer, not on engineering

Open questions carry these: whether treasury paper sits at MCDR or at the Central Bank through a bank custodian (which decides whether a bank custody account is needed at all), one FIX session per broker or one platform session identifying the broker in the message, whether indicative price feeds exist, Egyptian data residency, and the tax treatment. The commercial ones are a signed broker agreement with a licence number, bank letters of intent to certify, and named eKYC and AML providers.

One caution on how this is described

The application pack states fully digital onboarding, with ID capture and liveness, automated screening and unified-code registration, among the innovations. On the evidence above those three are interfaces, not capability. Describe them that way in the submission rather than relying on a later paragraph to qualify it: a regulator who finds the difference later will weigh it against everything else in the pack, and there is no advantage in the stronger wording. The criteria accept a solution still under development.

5. Test coverage

  • About 100 API unit tests across 17 files (ledger rules, pricing, projection, economics, order state, income, cash, onboarding rules, OTP and its delivery, compliance flags, audit, documents, campaign) and 3 FIX gateway test classes including a real logon over the protocol.
  • One end-to-end test asserting 25 checks against a fresh database with the real FIX gateway and bank simulator running: onboarding and one-time codes, compliance, deposit, quote, buy and sell over FIX, a trial balance that nets to zero, settlement, withdrawal with step-up and maker-checker, coupons and maturity with tax withheld, economics overrides, statement reconciliation, flags, audit trail, database-enforced tenant isolation, documents and the campaign. It runs in CI on every change.
  • Browser checks of the apps at phone and desktop widths.
  • Not covered, and worth stating: every external boundary, because the other side is the stand-in. Also partial fills, cancels and busts; recovery after a real network loss; dates around public holidays, since there is no calendar to test against; and load, failure-injection and security testing. The end-to-end run also uses the demo switches, so the production behaviour of those two flags is not the behaviour under test.

6. Phase map

  1. Phase 1 — Demo & sell (done). Hosted demo, branded prospect demos, economics, client experience, monitoring and audit, partnership documents, the retail campaign and its console.
  2. Phase 2 — Pilot readiness. KYC evidence capture, holiday calendar, tax sign-off, real SMS, deposit statement import, managed keys, observability and backups, security review, broker legal documents, demo switches off.
  3. Phase 3 — First live broker. FIX certification with 1–2 banks, production eKYC/AML, custodian reconciliation, invited clients, daily operations per the SOP.
  4. Phase 4 — Scale. More banks and brokers, regulatory reporting packs, recurring plans, mobile apps, disaster recovery.

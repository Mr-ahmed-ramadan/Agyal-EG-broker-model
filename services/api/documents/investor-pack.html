Fixed income for every Egyptian investor

The technology that lets any licensed broker sell T-bills and bonds online.

Agyal is a white-label, multi-tenant platform. Brokers bring the licence and the clients; banks bring the papers over FIX; Agyal runs everything in between and earns a share of the yield on every trade.

1. The problem

  • Retail investors are locked out. Egyptian treasury bills and bonds pay some of the highest yields in the region, yet individuals mostly reach them through bank branches, paper forms and minimums that suit institutions, not people.
  • Brokers can't build it themselves. Offering fixed income online needs digital onboarding (eKYC/AML), bank connectivity for pricing, a client-money ledger, custody reconciliation and coupon processing. Few brokerage firms have the engineering team or the time.
  • Deposits are the default. Without an easy alternative, savers stay in bank deposits that typically pay less after tax than the government paper those same banks hold.

2. The product

Client app

Broker-branded, Arabic and English, phone first. Online account opening, today's rates, what a paper earns after tax, live bank quotes, holdings, income, statements.

Broker console

Compliance queue with the broker's own approval rules, unified codes and custody, deposits, orders, settlement, coupons, withdrawals with maker-checker, ledger, economics.

Agyal platform

FIX 4.4 connectivity to every partner bank, best-price routing, double-entry ledger, KYC/AML monitoring across brokers, full audit trail, per-broker economics.

4. Go-to-market: through brokers

  1. Win brokers, not clients. Each brokerage firm brings an existing, already-licensed client base and its own brand. We never spend on retail acquisition.
  2. Branded demo in a day. The admin console creates a prospect demo with the broker's logo and colours; the partnership deck is sent as a tracked link.
  3. Pilot in 8–12 weeks. One or two partner banks over FIX, the broker's client-money and custody accounts, an invited first group of clients.
  4. Expand. More banks and instruments per broker, then more brokers on the same platform — each added broker is configuration, not a new build.

Operations SOP

The client lifecycle end to end: who does what, in which screen, and the checks at each step. Applies to every broker on the platform. Broker staff act in the broker console; Agyal staff in the admin console.

Daily checklist

  • 08:30 Agyal ops: admin → FIX — every bank session logged on; no stuck outbox messages.
  • 09:00 Operations: download the client-money account statement; match deposits (Deposits screen).
  • 09:30 Compliance: clear the compliance queue — Agyal flags first, then oldest applications.
  • During market hours Dealer: watch Orders for rejections or quotes not answered.
  • 14:00 Operations: confirm today's settlements against the custodian; confirm coupons and maturities due.
  • 15:00 Finance: approve and pay withdrawals (payment by a second person).
  • End of day Finance: Ledger screen — client money and securities reconcile to zero per currency and per ISIN.

Client lifecycle

1 · Onboarding

Client signs up with phone and email (SMS code), uploads national ID and selfie, answers suitability, accepts terms. System runs eKYC and AML screening and applies the broker's auto-approval rules.

Compliance opens each queued application (Compliance screen): check face match and ID result, AML hits, risk rating, suitability. Decide approve, request info or reject with a note.

Controls: every PEP goes to manual review; high risk needs a note; decisions are recorded against the named user. Target: decision within 1 business day. Agyal ops sees queue age in admin → KYC / AML and raises a Review overdue flag after 3 days.

2 · Unified code and custody

Operations (Unified codes screen) verifies the client's MCDR unified code, records the CBE (government papers) and MCDR (corporate) custody accounts. The client cannot trade until this is complete.

Controls: the name on the unified code must match the verified ID; mismatches go back to compliance.

3 · Deposits

Client transfers to the broker's segregated client account quoting their personal reference. Operations matches the bank-statement line to the pending deposit and confirms; the client's cash balance updates immediately.

Controls: amount and reference must match; transfers from a third party are returned, not credited; unmatched lines are investigated same day.

4 · Trading

Client requests a live price; a FIX QuoteRequest goes to every partner bank. The best quote is shown with the full cost breakdown; the client confirms. Cash (buy) or holdings (sell) are reserved before the order is sent.

Dealer monitors Orders: bank rejections, expired quotes, partial fills. Escalate repeated rejections from one bank to Agyal ops.

Controls: suitability limits the papers a client can buy; minimum rating A- for corporate paper; each fill posts to the ledger with broker margin, Agyal margin and custody fee booked separately.

5 · Settlement

Operations (Settlements screen) confirms each filled order once the custodian shows the paper registered to the client's code (buys) or delivered (sells). Sale proceeds become withdrawable only after settlement.

Controls: any trade unsettled at T+2 is escalated to the bank and to Agyal ops.

6 · Coupons and maturities

Operations (Income screen) confirms each coupon or redemption after the custodian credits the broker. Clients are credited automatically, net of withholding tax; matured holdings close.

Controls: total credited to clients plus tax withheld must equal the amount received from the custodian.

7 · Withdrawals

Client requests a withdrawal to an IBAN in their own name, confirmed with an SMS code. Finance approves; a different finance user marks it paid after the bank transfer.

Controls: only settled cash is withdrawable; new or changed IBANs need a step-up code; the platform refuses approve and pay by the same user.

8 · Margins and fees payout (monthly)

Finance reviews the Economics screen: broker revenue, Agyal platform fee payable and custody fee payable. Pays Agyal and the custodian from the broker's own account — never from the client-money account.

Controls: payable balances in the ledger must match the invoices.

9 · Ongoing monitoring

Compliance completes periodic reviews by the due date (set from the risk rating). Agyal ops reviews admin → KYC / AML and Audit log weekly and raises flags; the broker answers and resolves each flag in its Compliance screen.

Controls: open flags older than 5 business days are escalated to the broker's compliance head.

How it works

From sign-up to settled trade, fully online

1 · Onboard

National ID and selfie (eKYC), AML and PEP screening, suitability, MCDR unified code, e-signed agreements. Clean applications are approved by your rules; the rest go to your compliance queue.

2 · Price & trade

The client asks for a price; a FIX QuoteRequest goes to every partner bank. The best quote is shown with its full cost and what it earns to maturity. One tap sends the order.

3 · Run the book

Double-entry ledger, settlement confirmation, coupons and maturities, withdrawals with maker-checker, statements and a complete audit trail — in your broker console.

T-bills, treasury bonds and sovereign sukuk, plus corporate bonds, bills and sukuk rated A- or better. Arabic and English, phone first.

Next steps

A three-step path to your first live trade

  1. Branded demo & MOU — 2 weeks. Your brand on the demo, walkthrough with your compliance and operations leads, sign the memorandum of understanding.
  2. Pilot — 8 to 12 weeks. Connect one or two partner banks over FIX, your client-money and custody accounts, your eKYC/AML provider and SMS sender; invite a first group of clients.
  3. Launch. Open to all your clients, add banks and instruments, and review economics together after the first quarter.
What we need from you: a sponsor, a compliance and an operations contact, your bank relationships, and your approval of client-facing terms.

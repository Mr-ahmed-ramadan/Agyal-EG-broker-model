Slide 1 · Opener

1 min
“Offer treasury bills, bonds and sukuk to your clients — under your own brand, in weeks.”

“You already have the licence and the clients. What most firms don't have is the technology to offer fixed income online — onboarding, pricing from the banks, the ledger, settlement. That is exactly what we provide. You stay the licensed party; we are the technology behind you.”

Ask early: “Do your clients ask about T-bills today? Where do they buy them?” — let them describe the pain.

Slide 2 · Your questions, answered

3 min
Six questions: technology team · whose clients · where the papers come from · where the money is · cost · time to live.

“These are the six questions every broker asks us. Let me take the two that matter most to you.” Pick the two they reacted to on slide 1; don't read all six.

  • Papers: banks are primary dealers; they buy at CBE auctions or hold corporate issues. We ask all your partner banks at once; the client gets the best price.
  • Money and custody: cash never touches Agyal. It sits in your segregated client account; papers are registered to the client's unified code at the CBE (government) or MCDR (corporate).

Questions and answers

Is this licensed by the FRA?
The broker holds the licence and is the regulated party for every client and trade. Agyal is a technology provider under a services agreement; we support the broker's regulatory reporting and inspections.
What if a bank doesn't support FIX?
FIX is our standard path. For banks without FIX we can route requests through a bank portal (on the roadmap); we recommend starting the pilot with FIX-capable banks.
Who does eKYC and AML screening?
A licensed provider chosen with the broker (we integrate it); results flow into the broker's own approval rules and compliance queue. The demo uses a simulated provider.
Where is data hosted, and who owns it?
Client data belongs to the broker. It is stored encrypted, separated per broker at the database level, with a full audit trail. Hosting location is agreed in the MOU to meet data-protection requirements (Law 151/2020).
Can clients sell before maturity?
Yes — banks bid for the holding and the client sees net proceeds before confirming.
How do clients add and withdraw cash?
Bank transfer to the broker's client account with a personal reference; withdrawals only to an IBAN in the client's own name, confirmed by SMS code and approved by two different people (maker-checker).
What happens at a coupon or maturity?
Your operations confirm receipt from the custodian; clients are credited automatically, net of tax, and matured holdings close.
What does Agyal need from us to start?
A sponsor, compliance and operations contacts, introductions to your partner banks, your client-money and custody account details, and approval of client-facing terms.

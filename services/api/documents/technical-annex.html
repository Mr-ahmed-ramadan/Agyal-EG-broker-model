Technical annex

For the broker's IT, compliance and operations teams. Describes the platform, integrations, controls and what the broker provides. Contains no credentials.

1. Architecture

Client appBroker-branded web app, Arabic/English, phone first
Broker consoleCompliance, operations, finance, dealer roles
Platform APIOnboarding, pricing, orders, ledger, income, cash
FIX gatewayOne FIX 4.4 session per partner bank
Partner banksQuotes and executions over FIX
  • Multi-tenant by design: each broker's data is separated in the database itself (PostgreSQL row-level security), not only in application code.
  • Reliable messaging: the API and the FIX gateway exchange messages through transactional outbox/inbox tables, so no order or execution is lost or processed twice.
  • Double-entry ledger: every money and position movement is a balanced journal entry; client money and securities reconcile to zero per currency and per ISIN.

Per-bank rules of engagement (session IDs, party roles, trading hours, settlement conventions) are agreed with each bank and recorded before go-live. Certification with each bank's FIX team is part of the pilot.

3. Onboarding and compliance

This section describes the design. The identity and screening providers are chosen with the broker and are connected to stand-ins until then, and evidence capture is built to the standard the broker's compliance function sets. The capability audit states exactly what is live today.

  • Identity (eKYC): national ID and liveness check through a licensed provider agreed with the broker; the result is stored with the application, and the evidence retained with it is whatever the broker's policy and the Authority require.
  • AML screening: PEP, sanctions and adverse-media screening; risk rating LOW/MEDIUM/HIGH.
  • Suitability: investment horizon, loss tolerance and experience determine which paper types a client may buy.
  • Unified code: the client's MCDR unified code is verified by the broker's operations, and CBE/MCDR custody accounts are recorded before the first trade.
  • Decisions: the broker's auto-approval rules clear low-risk applications; all others (including every PEP) go to the broker's compliance queue. Every decision is recorded against a named user. Agyal monitors across brokers and can raise flags; it cannot approve or reject.
  • Periodic review: review dates are set by risk rating and tracked.

4. Client money and settlement

  • Client cash sits only in the broker's segregated client account. Deposits are matched by a personal reference and confirmed by operations.
  • Buys reserve cash before the order; sale proceeds can be reinvested immediately but withdrawn only after the bank settles.
  • Withdrawals go only to an IBAN in the client's own name, are confirmed by an SMS code, approved by finance and paid by a different person (maker-checker).
  • Coupons and redemptions are confirmed by operations against the custodian; clients are credited net of withholding tax.
  • Custody, broker margin and Agyal margin are booked to separate accounts on each fill and paid out separately, so the client-money account always equals what clients own.

5. Security controls

AreaControl
AuthenticationPassword plus one-time SMS code on every sign-in; step-up codes for sensitive actions (bank account, withdrawals). Codes are hashed, expire in 5 minutes and are rate-limited.
AuthorisationRole-based access for broker staff (admin, compliance, operations, dealer, finance); tokens are bound to one broker.
Data separationPostgreSQL row-level security per broker; platform-level access only for Agyal administrators.
EncryptionTLS in transit; national ID numbers encrypted at field level; only the last four digits are displayed.
AuditEvery state-changing request, sign-in attempt and administrator view of personal data is logged with user, IP and device; every data change is recorded by the database with before/after values. Secrets are never logged.
Change controlCode changes go through automated unit, integration and end-to-end tests before deployment.

6. Hosting and operations

  • Managed cloud hosting with automated deployments, daily database backups and health monitoring. Production hosting region and data-residency requirements are agreed with the broker in the definitive agreement.
  • Service levels (availability during Egyptian market hours, support response times, incident notification) are set out in the definitive agreement.

7. What the broker provides

  • Named sponsor and compliance, operations and finance contacts.
  • Introductions to partner banks' FIX / e-trading desks and agreement of rules of engagement.
  • Segregated client-money account and custody account details (CBE, MCDR).
  • Choice of eKYC/AML provider and SMS sender name (or use of Agyal's contracted providers).
  • Client-facing documents: terms, risk disclosure, privacy notice, e-sign agreement.
  • Branding: name in Arabic and English, logo, colours, domain for the client app.
